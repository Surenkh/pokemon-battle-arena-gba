# Meet the fighters

All thirteen Pokémon have distinct traits and elemental attacks. Pikachu, Squirtle, Jigglypuff and Gastly also have extra techniques you can learn in Practice. See the [technique guide](TECHNIQUES_PLAYER_GUIDE.md) for inputs, costs and counters.

| Pokémon | Fighting style | Attack visuals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pikachu | Quick Step lunges forward during melee startup; Volt Step adds a short dash. | Crackling lightning |
| Charmander | Ember Pressure drains more guard stamina. | Flames and embers |
| Squirtle | Efficient guard and stronger water pushback; Shell Roll adds movement to a squeeze. | Water pulses and splashes |
| Bulbasaur | Longer melee reach with a vine cue. | Spinning leaves and vines |
| Eevee | Can follow a clean melee hit with A or B. | Glowing stars |
| Meowth | Extra melee damage when catching attack recovery. | Claw waves and scratches |
| Psyduck | Mind Push gives strong projectile knockback. | Psychic rings and pulses |
| Jigglypuff | Air Brake changes landing timing once per jump. | Musical float notes and crescendos |
| Machop | Heavy Hands drains extra melee guard stamina. | Fists and impact bursts |
| Vulpix | Fox Step adds forward movement during melee startup. | Twin flames |
| Gastly | A second B arms a delayed, blockable orb burst. | Outlined shadow warning and purple blast |
| Riolu | Aura Punish adds damage against recovery. | Aura diamonds |
| Totodile | Jaw Pressure taxes guard stamina. | Water fangs |
