# Pokémon Battle Arena v1.1.0 — Selection & Power

- All thirteen Pokémon on one screen, with full card names and an animated
  selected portrait. No roster paging.
- Clear Difficulty and Location options, precise focus and a matching arena
  preview. Guide and Back retain the selected fighter and settings.
- Larger eight-frame super shots with moving elemental ribbons, orbiting
  charge effects and expanding impact bursts.
- Six-frame power-punch animation on A, aligned to the existing melee timing.
- Existing combat rules, CPU tuning, controls and schema-4 saves remain compatible.
