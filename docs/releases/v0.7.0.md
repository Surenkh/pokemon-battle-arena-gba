# Pokémon Battle Arena v0.7.0 — Sound & Poké Ball Recall

The title and menus now have an original looping tune, with new navigation, countdown, pause, result and recall cues. Music and sound effects have separate On/Off controls and a moderate default mix.

Normal fights now end with a Poké Ball recalling the defeated Pokémon: it arrives, opens, draws the fighter into a beam, closes and settles before results. The winner remains visible. Defeat results retain the closed ball; practice keeps its quick reset.

## Controls

- L on title, selector, practice setup, result or pause: Audio options.
- Up/Down selects Music or Sound FX; A/Left/Right toggles; B/Start/L returns.
- Audio choices remain selected during the current run and reset at power-on.
- A/Start after the ball closes skips the end of the recall.

Existing battle controls and fighter mechanics are unchanged. Music plays in menus; combat retains the elemental attack mix. Device/emulator volume is separate from the moderate in-game gains.

## Validation

Host checks cover presentation timing, all fighters/outcomes, bounds and combat-state isolation. Native mGBA checks cover looping music, independently disabled buses, actual digital silence while muted, both win/loss recall animations, sprite visibility, sound mix, pause, practice, effects and all CPU difficulties. A clean rebuild and ROM-bound reports are included under docs/verification/v0.7.0/. See docs/AUDIO_AND_RECALL_TEST_REPORT.md for exact results.

Physical GBA hardware and non-Linux rebuilding remain unverified. Unofficial fan project; preserve the artwork and engine credits.
