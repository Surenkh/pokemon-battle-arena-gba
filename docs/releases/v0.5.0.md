# Pokémon Battle Arena v0.5.0 — Fighter Depth & Practice

Six fighter traits now change how battles play: Pikachu steps into melee, Charmander pressures guard, Squirtle blocks efficiently and pushes with water, Bulbasaur reaches farther, Eevee links melee or fire, and Meowth punishes attack recovery.

Land a clean melee hit and use the FOLLOW cue to link one ordinary attack. Follow-ups spend normal stamina and remain blockable. Eevee's second melee needs close spacing. Resource recovery begins sooner, and the CPU uses actual reach, shared follow-up rules and a bounded stamina recovery policy.

B on the title opens practice. Choose an idle, blocking or attacking rival, use R for a fighter guide, and Select to change setup options or reset the active arena. Practice starts with full SP, disables timeout, shows damage/sequence feedback and automatically restarts after KO. Pause and ordinary battles retain their usual controls.

The release includes the native ROM, editable GBA Studio source, configuration, tools, tests and validation reports. See docs/FIGHTER_DEPTH_TEST_REPORT.md for measured results and checksum. CPU simulation results do not establish human matchup fairness; hardware and non-Linux builds remain unverified.
