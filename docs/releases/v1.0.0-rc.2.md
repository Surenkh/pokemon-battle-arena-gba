# Pokémon Battle Arena v1.0.0-rc.2 — Roster & Arena Expansion

Vulpix, Gastly, Riolu and Totodile join the roster, each with a distinct trait,
complete animation set, themed attacks/sounds, CPU style, guide, records and
three mastery medals. Frostpeak and Ancient Ruins bring the stage count to five.

- Two-page selection keeps all thirteen Pokémon readable. Left/Right crosses
  pages; Select opens the highlighted fighter's settings.
- Quick Battle and Practice select all five arenas. A full Arcade run rotates
  through all five, keeping its current stage on a retry.
- Schema-4 saves migrate earlier records, mastery and audio into independent
  new slots. The original legacy copies remain untouched.
- First-frame input acceptance, visible second-frame response, fast Practice
  retries and original-nine matchup tuning are retained.

This is a prerelease. Human matchup review, uncoached player pilots and physical
GBA/controller testing remain open. Latest stable remains v0.12.0. Preserve your
emulator's complete save file. No commercial ROM or BIOS is included.
