# Pokémon Battle Arena v0.4.0 — Combat Feel

Attacks now sound different for all six Pokémon. Shots, impacts, charging and super releases have electricity, fire, water, leaf, star or claw themes. Hits flash and shake the arena briefly while the HUD stays steady.

Blocks, guard breaks and exhausted stamina have clearer visual and audio cues. Supers show a themed charging glyph, rising sound pulses and a reminder to jump or squeeze. An Up press just before landing can now become a jump through the existing input queue.

Damage, stamina costs, attack timing, CPU decisions and super evasion rules retain their prior values. The update includes the downloadable ROM, editable GBA Studio source, deterministic sound generator and regression tests. See docs/COMBAT_FEEL_TEST_REPORT.md for validation and limitations. Physical hardware and non-Linux rebuilding remain unverified.
