# Pokémon Battle Arena v0.9.0 — Arenas & Fighter Mastery

- Three playable arenas: Woodland, Volcanic and Shoreline. New original pixel
  scenery uses subtle smoke/water motion and the same predictable combat floor.
- Choose an arena in Quick Battle or Practice. Arcade rotates across all three;
  retries keep the current arena. All setup controls use D-pad and A/Start.
- A visible Fighter Mastery menu tracks three cosmetic medals for each Pokémon:
  10 damaging follow-up hits, 5 successful super evasions, and a no-continue
  Arcade clear at any difficulty. Practice cannot farm mastery.
- Roster badges display earned medals; round results celebrate a new medal.
  Mastery and Records link directly with A.
- Checked dual-copy schema-3 SRAM preserves earlier six- and nine-fighter saves,
  records and audio settings. Existing no-continue clears already count.
- The nine-fighter combat ruleset, title music and Poké Ball recall are retained.

Keep your emulator save file. Mastery saves at round end, Rematch or leaving a
fight. Abrupt power-off during a battle can lose its unsaved steps. Unfinished
Arcade runs do not resume after power-off. Human balance playtesting, physical
GBA hardware and non-Linux rebuilding remain unverified. Unofficial fan project.
