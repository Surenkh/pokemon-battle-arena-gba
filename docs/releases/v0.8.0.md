# Pokémon Battle Arena v0.8.0 — Modes & New Challengers

- Visible main-menu buttons: Quick Battle, Arcade, Practice, Records, Audio Settings and Controls. Pick a mode first, then a fighter/difficulty or practice variation. Menu entry uses D-pad and A/Start; no R shortcut is required.
- Nine selectable Pokémon in a 3×3 grid. Psyduck, Jigglypuff and Machop add distinct stats, traits, animation sets, projectile/super/impact graphics, combat audio and CPU styles.
- Psyduck pushes rivals with psychic pulses. Jigglypuff has lighter gravity and longer jumps. Machop adds melee guard pressure.
- Five-rival Arcade samples unique opponents from the other eight fighters, keeps the chosen difficulty and offers two continues. Resources reset between fights. Draws retry freely; pause Rematch spends a continue.
- Champion/Records save clears, no-continue clears and fastest total battle times across 27 fighter/difficulty pairs. Audio settings also persist.
- Checked dual-copy SRAM migrates earlier six-fighter v0.8 records without losing existing entries. A damaged newer save falls back to the earlier valid copy.
- Original title music, combat feel, Practice, Poké Ball recall and subtle canopy motion remain included. The title clearly identifies v0.8.0 and nine fighters.

Keep your emulator save file. Unfinished runs do not resume after power-off. Timing counts active battle ticks, including failed attempts, and excludes menus/countdown/recall. Physical GBA hardware and non-Linux builds remain untested. This is an unofficial fan project.
