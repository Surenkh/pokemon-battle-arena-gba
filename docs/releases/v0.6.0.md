# Pokémon Battle Arena v0.6.0 — Smarter & More Varied Opponents

The six Pokémon now fight with distinct tactics. Pikachu presses close, Charmander threatens guard with fire, Squirtle keeps space with water and efficient blocks, Bulbasaur uses its reach, Eevee looks for follow-ups, and Meowth seeks recovery punishes.

- New fighter-aware spacing, short tactical plans and resource reserves.
- More purposeful block, jump and squeeze decisions; reachable whiffs and super startup can be counterattacked.
- Three distinct difficulty profiles: Easy leaves more openings; Normal mixes plans; Hard reacts and follows up more reliably.
- Short memory of repeated visible attacks, deliberate mistakes and a close approach to break stalled zoning.
- Compact public-only observation history. All opponents use the same combat rules, costs, damage and movement as the player.

## Play

Open `PokemonBattleArena.gba` in a GBA emulator. Choose Easy, Normal or Hard in the selector's LEVEL row. Controls and practice are unchanged: A melee, B fire, L block, Up jump, Down squeeze, R super at 100 SP and 25 stamina, Start pause. B on the title opens practice; R in a selector opens the fighter guide.

## Validation

Host checks cover deterministic combat, movement, feedback, practice and CPU behavior. Native mGBA checks cover all fighters and difficulty levels, effects/audio, pause, complete rounds and practice. The release includes ROM-bound reports and an identical clean-build check in `docs/verification/v0.6.0/`. See `docs/CPU_TACTICS_TEST_REPORT.md` for measured results.

No fighter stat changes were made. CPU comparisons guide tactics tuning; human matchup balance and physical GBA hardware testing remain outstanding. Arena motion and arcade progression remain planned for a later update.
