# Pokémon Battle Arena v0.12.0 — Match Goals & Rival Scouting

Quick Battle, Arcade results and Champion now show one relevant mastery line.
A new medal takes priority; otherwise it shows an actual increment from that
fight or the next unfinished goal. All three medals earned gets a completed
message. The existing medal notice and save warning remain available.

Arcade names the actual next or retry rival and gives one short counter cue
based on its authored trait. All nine rivals have their own cue. Champion and
exhausted runs show no impossible next rival. A/Start continues directly with
no additional screen or mandatory wait.

This is a playable prototype for the roadmap's post-match motivation idea.
Player understanding and replay motivation remain subject to the five-player
pilot. Combat rules, save schema and Practice mastery exclusion are unchanged.
