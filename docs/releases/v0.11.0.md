# Pokémon Battle Arena v0.11.0 — Practice & Drills

Practice now offers Follow-up and Evade Super through its Training setting.
Follow-up starts in melee range and coaches a clean A into a damaging B.
Evade Super gives the rival one normally charged super and checks a nearby jump
or timed squeeze through its full passage. Short coaching messages use actual
combat events. Free, Block and Attack modes are also available.

Select retries with restored resources and positions. A fresh action works on
emulator frame 8 after a manual reset. Practice KOs use a six-frame hold and
six-frame Ready, accepting a fresh action on frame 14 while retaining statistics.
Buttons pressed during Ready cannot trigger later as stale attacks.

Both drills support all nine fighters and the three arenas. Practice contributes
to no mastery counters.

## Validation

All 12 native suites pass with 14,939 checks and zero missed display frames.
The Practice runner completes both lessons, tests both evasions for all fighters,
measures 46 manual resets and a real KO restart, checks pause and verifies
mastery exclusion. Ten host suites, art/audio checks, a 4,860-match comparison
and the 90 tap-response trials pass. A separate clean build reproduces the ROM.
